What's new

La Cigale 1.7

An AI assistant that runs on your computer, with no account and without sending your pages to anyone. It summarizes, explains, translates, and can now stay open next to your tabs.

Summarize this page in three points

On this computer

01

A local AI assistant

The model downloads once (2.5 to 5 GB depending on your choice), then everything happens on your computer: no question and no page goes out to the Internet. It can read the open tab and, if you allow it, your tabs, notes and bookmarks.

  • Ask La Cigale
  • Summarize this page
  • Analyze the links
  • Translate the page
  • Explain the selection

02

Ask La Cigale

Right-clicking a page or a tab replaces the old "Ask an AI chatbot": the request goes to your local assistant, not to an online service.

03

The assistant beside the page

It opens in a panel on the side of the window and follows the tab you are looking at. It has its own icon in the sidebar.

La cigale chante tout l'été The cicada sings all summer

04

Local translation

"Translate the page" replaces the text in place, top of the page first, keeping links and formatting. One button brings back the original. It is slower than an online service, but nothing leaves.

  1. Thinking
  2. Checking
  3. Answer

05

A model that thinks

"Thinker" (Qwen3 4B) reasons before answering, and you can read its thinking step by step.

  • Bread recipeyesterday
  • Letter to the landlordMonday
  • Article summaryNov 12

06

History, if you want it

Off by default. When on, it keeps your last 50 conversations on this computer. The panic button wipes them along with everything else.

And small touches
