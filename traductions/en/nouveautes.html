La Cigale 1.8

A complete redesign in liquid glass: capsule tabs, a translucent address bar and menus, glass pages, a single typeface and controls that respond. Your settings and protections stay the same.

01

A liquid glass frame

The open tab becomes a glass capsule, the address bar gets a blue ring while you type, and the page sits in a frame with rounded corners. The light theme has white glass, the olive theme green glass.

02

Glass pages

The home page, Protection, the assistant and these pages move to frosted glass: translucent cards, a light edge, soft shadows, a blue main button. With the olive theme, the accent stays olive.

03

Controls that respond

Tabs slide open, buttons press in under the click, cards ease into place. If Windows is set to reduce animations, they stop.

The system typeface

Titles, numbers and text use the Windows typeface, set tight like on a Mac. No more mix of two families.
