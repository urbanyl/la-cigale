Privacy

Last updated 23 November 2026.

In short

  • The La Cigale project collects no personal data: no account, no server of its own, no telemetry, no analytics, no cookies.
  • Since nothing is collected, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete.

This website

  • It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in its privacy statement. The publisher has no access to these logs.
  • No cookies, no tracking scripts. The font is served by the site itself, with no call to Google or any other service.
  • Downloads come from GitHub Releases, under the same terms.

The browser

  • Browsing data (history, cookies, cache) stays on your computer and is wiped on close, unless you change that.
  • Quad9 (Switzerland) resolves domain names over encrypted DNS: it sees the domains you visit, without linking them to an account.
  • GitHub, once a day, to check for a new version (can be turned off on the Protection page).
  • uBlock Origin downloads its filter lists from their authors.
  • Mozilla, only when you translate a page or install an extension from its site.
  • api.ipify.org, only when you test your IP address.
  • Your shortcuts' websites, to show their logo.
  • Your proxy provider or the Tor network, only if you turn them on: they then see the sites you visit, under their own terms.

Your rights

  • For any question about your data: through a GitHub issue, or privately through security reporting.
  • You can also contact the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or your own.
